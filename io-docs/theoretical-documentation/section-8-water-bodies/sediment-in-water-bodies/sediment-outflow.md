# Sediment Outflow

The amount of sediment transported out of the water body on a given day is calculated as a function of the final concentration. The initial suspended solid concentration is:

&#x20;          $$sed_{flowout}=conc_{sed,f}*V_{flowout}$$                                           8:2.3.1

where $$sed_{flowout}$$ is the amount of sediment transported out of the water body with outflow (metric tons), $$conc_{sed,f}$$ is the final sediment concentration in the water body (Mg/m$$^3$$), and $$V_{flowout}$$ is the volume of outflow from the impoundment (m$$^3$$ H$$_2$$O).
