# depos

**Code reference:** `topo_db(ith)%dep_co` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/topography_data_module.f90#L11)).

This parameter is used to compute the sediment transport capacity of surface runoff across an HRU. A large coefficient will result in a high transport capacity and thus a larger amount of sediment leaving the HRU.&#x20;
