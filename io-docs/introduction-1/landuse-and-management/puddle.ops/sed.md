# sed

**Code reference:** `pudl_db(ic)%sed` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/mgt_operations_module.f90#L20)).

This value sets the sediment concentration in the HRU water storage when the puddling operation is performed. The model combines the concentration with the water volume to determine the sediment amount.
