# phuacc

**Code reference:** `transpl(ic)%phuacc` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/plant_data_module.f90#L158)).

Fraction of annual plant heat units accumulated at transplanting. It is used to initialize the crop phenological heat-unit state.
