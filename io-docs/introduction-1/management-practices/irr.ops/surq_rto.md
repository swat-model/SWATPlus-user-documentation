# sumq\_frac

**Code reference:** `irrop_db(irr_op)%surq` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/mgt_operations_module.f90#L9)).

Dimensionless fraction used to divide irrigation water between water applied to the land and surface runoff. Enter a value from 0.0 to 1.0.
