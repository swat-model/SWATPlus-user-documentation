# n\_conc

**Code reference:** `sd_chd(idb)%n_conc` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L39)).

Nitrogen concentration in channel bank and bed material, entered in mg/kg. SWAT+ uses it to calculate organic nitrogen associated with eroded bank and bed sediment.
