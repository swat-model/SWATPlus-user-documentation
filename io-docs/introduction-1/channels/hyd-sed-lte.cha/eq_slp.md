# eq\_slp

**Code reference:** `sd_chd(idb)%vcr_coef` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L30)).

This dimensionless coefficient multiplies the critical velocity calculated by the channel sediment routine. A value of 1 uses the calculated critical velocity unchanged.
