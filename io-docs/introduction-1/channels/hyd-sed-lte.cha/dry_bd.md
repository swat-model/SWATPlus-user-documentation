# dry\_bd

**Code reference:** `sd_chd(idb)%ch_bd` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/sd_channel_module.f90#L34)).

Dry bulk density of channel bank and bed material in t/m3. SWAT+ uses it in eroded-sediment mass calculations and in the channel-sediment porosity calculation.
