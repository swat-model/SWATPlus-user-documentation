# imax

**Code reference:** `imax / db_mx%wallo_pou` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_allocation_read.f90#L19), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_allocation_read.f90#L43-L44)).

Total number of place-of-use (POU) records in `place_of_use.wro` (stored in `db_mx%wallo_pou`). It allocates the `pou`, `poud_met`–`poua_met`, and `poud_om`–`poua_om` arrays in [`water_allocation_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_allocation_read.f90#L47-L55) and bounds daily POU loops in [`wallo_start_day.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/wallo_start_day.f90#L34,L95,L128), [`wallo_control.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/wallo_control.f90#L40,L57), and [`time_control.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/time_control.f90#L382).
