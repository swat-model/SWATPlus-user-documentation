# num_aqu

**Code reference:** `canal(ic)%num_aqu` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_allocation_module.f90#L210), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_canal_read.f90#L46,L55)).

Number of aquifers receiving seepage from this canal. Read first into local `num_aqu` ([`water_canal_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_canal_read.f90#L46)) to allocate `aqu_loss_fr` ([`water_canal_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_canal_read.f90#L51)), then reread into `canal(ic)%num_aqu` ([`water_canal_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_canal_read.f90#L55)). Bounds the aquifer seepage loop in [`wallo_canal.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/wallo_canal.f90#L38-L49).
