# num_aqu

**Code reference:** `canal(ic)%num_aqu` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_allocation_module.f90#L210)).

The number of aquifer seepage fractions supplied at the end of this canal row. Follow this field with exactly that many [aqu_loss_fr(iaq)](aqu_loss_fr_iaq.md) values.
