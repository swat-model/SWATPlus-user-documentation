# org_min_typ

**Code reference:** `wtp(iwtp)%conc(itrt)%org_min_typ` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_allocation_module.f90#L129), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_treatment_read.f90#L51)).

6-character resolution mode (`"const"`, `"dtbl"`, or `"recall"`; default: `""`). Dispatched during read in [`water_treatment_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_treatment_read.f90#L61-L88) and during treatment simulation in [`wallo_treatment.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/wallo_treatment.f90#L28-L57). Note that for `"dtbl"`, [`water_treatment_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/water_treatment_read.f90#L74) matches against `dtbl_flo`, whereas [`wallo_treatment.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/wallo_treatment.f90#L36) indexes `dtbl_lum(id)`.
