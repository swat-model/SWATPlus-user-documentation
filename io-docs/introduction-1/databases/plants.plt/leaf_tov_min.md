# leaf\_tov\_min

**Code reference:** `pldb(ic)%leaf_tov_min` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/plant_data_module.f90#L85)).

Minimum-stress perennial leaf-turnover time, in months. SWAT+ converts the stress-adjusted monthly turnover time to a daily turnover fraction as 1 / (30 × leaf_tov_mon).
