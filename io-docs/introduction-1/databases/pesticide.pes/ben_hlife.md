# ben\_hlife

**Code reference:** `pestdb(ip)%ben_hlife` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/pesticide_data_module.f90#L19)).

Half-life of the pesticide in the benthic (channel bed) sediment, in days. SWAT+ adjusts it for water temperature when it computes pesticide decay in the channel bed sediment.
