# bmix\_b

**Code reference:** `bmix_b` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/tillage_data_module.f90#L11), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/carbon_bsn_read.f90#L77), [calibration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/cal_parm_select.f90#L537-L538)).

Multiplier of the clay term $(b)$ in the equation [`mgt_tillfactor.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/mgt_tillfactor.f90#L59-L61) uses for biological mixing events (originally fixed with constants $3$, $5$, and $-5.5$):

$$
z = a + b \, e^{c \cdot \text{clay} / 100}, \qquad F = z \, \frac{x}{x + e^{1 - 2x}}
$$

where:

- $z$ is the largest factor the layer can reach (dimensionless),
- $a$ is [bmix\_a](bmix_a.md) (dimensionless),
- $b$ is `bmix_b` (dimensionless),
- $c$ is [bmix\_c](bmix_c.md) (dimensionless),
- $\text{clay}$ is the layer clay content (%),
- $F$ is the biological mixing factor (dimensionless), and
- $x$ is the mixing effectiveness of the event plus the factor left from earlier events (dimensionless).

The biological and tillage factors are added, and with [idc\_till](../../basin-1/codes.bsn/idc_till.md) `= 3` decomposition is multiplied by $1 + F$ ([`cbn_zhang2.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/cbn_zhang2.f90#L380-L383)).

The SWAT+ example file (`refdata/Ames_sub1`) heads this column `bmix_b` and uses 5.0. The calibration parameter is `bmix_b`.
