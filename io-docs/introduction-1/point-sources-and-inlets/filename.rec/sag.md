# sag

**Code reference:** `recall(i)%hd%sag` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/hydrograph_module.f90#L46), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/recall_read.f90#L158-L171)).

Detached small-aggregate load (`tons`; Fortran default: `0.`). Read into `recall(i)%hd` and routed to `ob(icmd)%hd(1)` during simulation in [`command.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/command.f90#L325-L344) (with subdaily inputs summed across steps in [`recall_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/recall_read.f90#L158-L160)).
