# K_zone

**Code reference:** `K_zone` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/gwflow_read.f90#L88), [read](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/gwflow_read.f90#L451)).

Zone number, that is the line position in [zones.gw](../zones.gw/README.md). The cell takes `aquifer_K` from this zone ([`gwflow_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/gwflow_read.f90#L461)). The same zone supplies `thermal_K` when heat transport is on ([`gwflow_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/gwflow_read.f90#L459), [`gwflow_read.f90`](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/gwflow_read.f90#L1493)). A number larger than the number of zones is outside the zone arrays and is not checked.
