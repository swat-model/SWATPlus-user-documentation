# lab\_p

**Code reference:** `solt_db(isolt)%lab_p` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/soil_data_module.f90#L16)).

Initial labile phosphorus concentration at the soil surface, in ppm. SWAT+ multiplies it by the layer depth factor to initialize labile phosphorus in each soil layer.
