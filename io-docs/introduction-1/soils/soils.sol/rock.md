# rock

**Code reference:** `soildb(isol)%ly(j)%rock` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/soil_data_module.f90#L55)).

The percent of the sample that has a particle diameter > 2 mm, i.e. the percent of the sample that does not pass through a 2 mm sieve.
