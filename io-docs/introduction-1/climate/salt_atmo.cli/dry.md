# dry

**Code reference:** `atmodep_salt(iadep)%salt(isalt)%dry` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/climate_module.f90#L225)).

Dry deposition over the year in kg/ha. SWAT+ divides by 365 to obtain the daily input. See [salt_rain.f90](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/salt_rain.f90#L42-L82).
