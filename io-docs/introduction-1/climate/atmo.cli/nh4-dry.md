# nh4_dry

Dry ammonium nitrogen deposition in kg/ha over the selected period. Daily dry load is $M/D$, where $M$ is the input and $D$ is the days in the month or 365 for yearly and average annual input.

See [nut_nrain.f90](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/nut_nrain.f90#L44-L65).
