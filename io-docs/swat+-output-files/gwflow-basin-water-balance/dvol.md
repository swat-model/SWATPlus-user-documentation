# dvol

**Code reference:** `gw_hyd_grid_mo%chng` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/gwflow_module.f90#L118)).

The net change in groundwater storage during the reporting period. It is accumulated from the daily after-storage minus before-storage values; it is not the total volume stored at the end of the period.

**Unit:** mm.

Monthly and yearly values sum the daily storage changes. Average-annual output gives the mean annual storage change over the reporting years.
