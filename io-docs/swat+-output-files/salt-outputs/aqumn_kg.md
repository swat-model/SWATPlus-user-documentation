# aqumn_kg

**Code reference:** `salt_basin(28)` ([calculation](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/salt_balance.f90#L354-L367)).

Solid salt stored in the standard aquifer objects' five mineral pools. The calculation assumes 15 m of aquifer thickness and a bulk density of 2,000 kg/m³.

**Unit:** kg. The longer periods report mean daily storage.

See [reporting periods](basin-salt-balance.md#reporting-periods) for aggregation.
