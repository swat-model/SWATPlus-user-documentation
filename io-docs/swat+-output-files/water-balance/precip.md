# precip

**Code reference:** `bwb_d%precip` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L6)).

Full-HRU precipitation uses the local weather record after any elevation adjustment. HRU-LTE copies precipitation from the station record. The column records precipitation before interception and the snowfall calculation. [Calculation and reporting details](README.md#snow-irrigation-and-drainage)

**Unit:** mm.
