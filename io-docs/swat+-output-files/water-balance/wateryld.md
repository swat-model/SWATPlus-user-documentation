# wateryld

**Code reference:** `bwb_d%wateryld` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L11)).

Full HRUs sum lagged surface runoff, lateral flow, and tile drainage with a floor of zero. HRU-LTE adds shallow groundwater discharge to lateral soil flow before the same components are summed. [Calculation and reporting details](README.md#run-on-and-water-yield)

**Unit:** mm.
