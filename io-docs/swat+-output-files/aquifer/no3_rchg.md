# no3_rchg

**Code reference:** `aqu_d(iaq)%no3_rchg` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/aquifer_module.f90#L48)).

Nitrate nitrogen entering the aquifer with recharge, expressed as nitrogen mass per unit area.

**Unit:** kg N/ha.

Daily output gives the daily exchange. Monthly and yearly output sum exchanges over the period. Average-annual output gives the mean annual total over the printed simulation years.

Basin output weights aquifer values by aquifer area relative to the total basin area.
