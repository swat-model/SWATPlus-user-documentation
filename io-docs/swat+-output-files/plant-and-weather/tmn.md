# tmn

**Code reference:** `bpw_d%tmn` ([declaration](https://github.com/swat-model/swatplus/blob/6f515368525e7d84089ae0f9d33f4c9115de5679/src/output_landscape_module.f90#L211)).

Daily minimum air temperature from the station after the monthly climate adjustment. Full HRUs also apply their elevation lapse adjustment when enabled. Longer-period rows average the daily values. [Daily calculations](plant-weather-output.md#daily-weather-and-heat-units)

**Unit:** deg C.
